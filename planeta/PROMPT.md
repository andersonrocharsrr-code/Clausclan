# Master prompt — "A Viagem ao Planeta dos Números" (2D flat animated music video)

Copy everything below the line into the video tool (or into Claude) together with the song file.
Timings were taken from the real song (2:30.8), word by word, so each action lands on its lyric.
The song's own counting order is used: 1 star, 2 moons, 1 comet with a hat, 3 planets, 4 astronauts,
5 stars, 6 aliens, 7 rockets, 8 robots, 9 stars, 10 planets.

---

## GLOBAL DIRECTION (applies to every shot)

Create a 2D flat-design animated children's music video, 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps, total length exactly 2:30.8,
synchronized to the attached song "A Viagem ao Planeta dos Números" (Brazilian Portuguese). Do not change,
cut, speed up or replace the audio.

**Style.** Clean vector art, minimalist rounded geometric shapes, no outlines or thin consistent outlines, soft
two-tone shading, subtle glow on light sources, no photorealism, no 3D, no gradients heavier than a soft sky fade.
Modern educational motion-graphics look.

**Palette.** Deep space navy background (#141A40 to #262B6B) with soft purple, pink and teal nebula clouds.
Accents: soft neon pink (#FF8FB8), mint green (#7EE0C0), warm yellow (#FFD86B), soft purple (#B79CF2),
turquoise (#3FC8D4), white (#F7F9FF). Never use harsh pure neon or pure black.

**Motion.** Smooth and playful: squash and stretch, elastic pop-ins with a small overshoot, gentle floating
(slow sine bob and slight rotation) for everything in zero gravity, eased camera moves, no hard cuts except
where stated. Background stars drift slowly to the left at three parallax depths the whole time we are in space,
with small geometric asteroids and tiny ringed planets passing by. Everything bounces lightly on the beat
(about 140 BPM).

**Recurring characters — keep identical in every shot.**
- **Hero astronaut:** a cheerful child, light skin, short brown hair, big round eyes, rosy cheeks; chubby white
  spacesuit with turquoise gloves, boots, belt and chest panel (three small buttons: pink, yellow, mint); round
  glass helmet with a white rim, turquoise ear caps and a small antenna with a pink ball.
- **Rocket:** short, chubby, white body, turquoise nose cone, pink side fins and pink belly band, one large round
  turquoise-rimmed window (the hero's face is visible in it when flying), orange-yellow-white flat flame.
- **Number graphics:** very big, bold, rounded digits with a thick white outline and a soft dark drop shadow,
  each digit in one accent color. They pop in with an elastic bounce exactly on the sung word and keep
  pulsing on the beat.
- **All creatures** have the same face style: two big white eyes with dark pupils and a white highlight, pink
  cheeks, small smile; mouths open and close with the singing.

**Text.** Only the digits, "?" and "!" marks, and the final message. No other on-screen text, no subtitles,
no watermarks, no logos.

**Negative prompt.** No realistic humans, no scary creatures, no weapons, no extra limbs or fingers, no character
redesign between shots, no flicker, no random text, no cluttered frames, no camera shake except at liftoff.
